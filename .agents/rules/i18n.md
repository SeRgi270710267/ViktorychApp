# i18n

- Every new or changed user-facing string goes into all four locale packs in `i18n/`: `en`, `es`, `ru`, `hi`. Real translations, not English copies.
- Wrap UI strings with `tr("...")`. Do not hard-code text in templates (`scripts/i18n_hardcoded.py` checks).
- `python3 scripts/check_i18n.py` must pass before pushing.
- On a merge conflict in `i18n/*.js` or `scripts/i18n_allow.txt`, keep both sides (main's entries first, then yours) and fix the comma between them.
- Titles shown on first-run and sign-up screens are translated too.
